How the plan works

Each entry gets one unused team per day for the rest of the season, chosen so the product of win chances is as high as possible. That spends teams whose only safe games are November and December buy games and saves the teams that are still big favorites in conference play. Entries are steered to different games on the same day. Win chances are the card's full model when the day has a card, otherwise the model's current ratings. Teams an entry has used never appear for it again. Pick records the choice (it refuses a used team or a game that has tipped); the page updates within about 10 minutes. Picks lock at tipoff, and a missed day ends an entry: pick every day.
