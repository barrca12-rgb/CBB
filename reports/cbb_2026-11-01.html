How the model works
RatingsOffense, defense and tempo for every team from every D-I box score this season, adjusted for opponent and home court, recent games weighted more. Each team starts from KenPom's preseason rating.
Final numberA second stage blends the ratings with KenPom and Barttorvik, the Four Factor matchups, rest and back-to-backs, each team's own home edge, and missing players (minutes from players who sat out the team's last game).
EdgeModel margin or total minus the best line across books. The model never sees the line, so the edge is a real disagreement.
Backtest2014-2026, walk-forward (every game predicted only from earlier games). No edge against closing lines; 57.5% ATS and 55-56% on totals against opening lines in 2024-26. Details in BACKTEST.md.
